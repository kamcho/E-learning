🚀 How it works

From signup to smarter revision

Four simple steps to get every child practising with confidence.

  1. 01 · Start

    Create your account

    Sign up as a guardian with your phone number and PIN — or use Google — in under a minute.

  2. 02 · Profile

    Add your learners

    Link each child with their grade (PP1–Grade 9) so exams and progress stay personal.

  3. 03 · Practise

    Practise KPSEA, KJSEA & KCBE

    Pick a subject, start a test, and revise for KPSEA, KJSEA, or KCBE with notes plus AI feedback that explains answers.

  4. 04 · Grow

    Track progress

    See scores, streaks, and weak areas on your dashboard — so you know what to revise next.

✦ About us

Education should inspire, not intimidate

Soma Smart exists to make learning and revision fun, interactive, and accessible for every child from PP1 to Grade 9. We believe CBE success comes from practice that builds confidence — not fear of exams.

Families use Soma Smart for KPSEA, KJSEA, and KCBE (Kenya Certificate of Basic Education) practice questions, clear notes, and AI-powered analysis that tutors learners through mistakes. Parents see real progress; children stay curious.

  • Built for Kenya’s Competency-Based Education (CBE)
  • KPSEA, KJSEA & KCBE practice that feels approachable
  • AI tutors that explain — they don’t just mark
  • Guardian dashboards made for busy parents

Who we serve

Learners — PP1 to Grade 9 revising at their own pace.

Guardians — tracking progress without the guesswork.

Teachers & partners — aligning practice to CBE strands, with a free class code to track every learner.

Schools — buy learner places, add teachers, and let every child join through a class link.

Our promise

Quality practice, honest feedback, and a learning space where every child can grow with confidence.
