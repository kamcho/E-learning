🚀 How it works

From signup to smarter revision

Four simple steps to get every child practising with confidence.

  1. 01

    Create your account

    Sign up as a guardian with your phone number and PIN — or use Google — in under a minute.

  2. 02

    Add your learners

    Link each child with their grade (PP1–Grade 9) so exams and progress stay personal.

  3. 03

    Practise KEPSEA & KJSEA

    Pick a subject, start a test, and revise with notes plus AI feedback that explains answers.

  4. 04

    Track progress

    See scores, streaks, and weak areas on your dashboard — so you know what to revise next.

✦ About us

Education should inspire, not intimidate

Soma Smart exists to make learning and revision fun, interactive, and accessible for every child from PP1 to Grade 9. We believe CBC success comes from practice that builds confidence — not fear of exams.

Families use Soma Smart for KEPSEA and KJSEA practice questions, clear notes, and AI-powered analysis that tutors learners through mistakes. Parents see real progress; children stay curious.

  • Built for Kenya’s Competency-Based Curriculum
  • KEPSEA & KJSEA practice that feels approachable
  • AI tutors that explain — they don’t just mark
  • Guardian dashboards made for busy parents

Who we serve

Learners — PP1 to Grade 9 revising at their own pace.

Guardians — tracking progress without the guesswork.

Teachers & partners — aligning practice to CBC strands.

Our promise

Quality practice, honest feedback, and a learning space where every child can grow with confidence.
