✦ Why SomaKenya

Everything a CBC Learner Needs

📚

Interactive Lessons

Rich multimedia lessons with videos, animations and quizzes covering all CBC strands and sub-strands.

📝

Smart Assessments

Formative and summative assessments that adapt to the learner's pace, aligned to CBC competency benchmarks.

📈

Progress Dashboard
