How billing works

Short answers before you pay.

How do I pay?

Choose a plan, enter the M-Pesa number, and approve the STK Push on your phone. Credits appear as soon as Safaricom confirms the payment.

Do unused tests roll over?

Credits stay on the account until the plan end date. After that unused tests expire with the subscription.

Can more than one learner use a pack?

Yes. Tests are shared across the learners linked to your guardian account, up to the plan’s learner limit.
